Chapter Bridge

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Terms of Service

Chapter Bridge is a personal ebook and audiobook sync app for Windows and Android. It is built by Eric Lyman, an individual developer, not a company. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What the app does on your device

Chapter Bridge aligns ebook and audiobook files you already own, word by word. It does that with Whisper transcription that runs locally on your device. Audio is never uploaded to any cloud service.

No analytics, tracking, or advertising

Chapter Bridge has no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. Eric operates no servers for the app and receives no user data.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive

The app uses Google Sign-In and Google Drive for one purpose: to store and sync your reading position and related app sync data in your own Google Drive, so your place carries across your devices.

That access is limited to the files and app data Chapter Bridge itself creates for syncing. Chapter Bridge does not read other content in your Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Chapter Bridge's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide the sync feature. It is not sold, not transferred to third parties, and not used for advertising. It is not read by humans except with your consent, for security, or as required by law.

Revoking access

You can disconnect Chapter Bridge at any time. Open your Google Account, go to Security, then third-party connections (Third-party apps & services), and remove Chapter Bridge.

Deleting synced data

Synced data stays in your Google Drive. Eric does not keep a copy. To delete it, delete the app's data in your Drive: the files and app data Chapter Bridge created for syncing.

Children

Chapter Bridge is not directed at children under 13.
