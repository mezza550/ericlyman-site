About

I build the operating systems that let businesses grow—people, process, technology, and the P&L that ties them together.

Most recently I have led a PE-backed community association management branch across a 95-association portfolio, taking it to profitability in the first year and introducing new workforce and AI models along the way. Before that I founded a real estate acquisition company, and spent a decade in product and operations leadership at Wayfair, LEGO, and DreamWorks Animation.

I am at my best in the work between strategy and execution: designing a model, piloting it, and scaling what works.

B.S., Media Arts and Animation, The Art Institute of Portland (Honors). Certified Manager of Community Associations (CMCA).
