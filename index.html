Operations & AI Transformation Leader

I love designing better processes and building them with AI. At RowCal that meant turning around a struggling branch, breaking property management work into new remote roles, and building AI agents that took error-prone manual work off the team. Outside work, I build agents people rely on daily, from a personal fitness coach to the back office of a freelance design business. I want to do this at a larger scale: finding the processes that should work differently, then designing and rolling out the AI-enabled version.
